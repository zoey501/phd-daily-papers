Science Advances · 2025 · Wu et al.

Translation landscape of stress granules

Relevance to your PhD: 10/10

Main question

What is the spatial organization and translational state of ribosomal machinery inside stress granules in intact cells?

Problem solved

Stress granules are linked to translational control, but conventional microscopy cannot directly resolve ribosomal complexes inside granules in their native three-dimensional environment.

Key findings

The study used cryogenic correlative light and electron microscopy plus tomography to quantify translational machinery inside stress granules. The results suggest that stress granules have limited impact on global translation regulation but may protect small ribosomal subunits and pre-initiation complexes from degradation.

Why it matters to your research

This directly connects stress granules, ribosomes, translation and cryo-CLEM. It is especially useful for thinking about whether drug-induced granules contain canonical 40S/pre-initiation machinery or an unusual ribosome composition.

What you can learn / use

  • Cryo-CLEM targeting strategy for stress granules.
  • Tomographic analysis of ribosomal particles inside versus outside granules.
  • A framework for comparing SG composition rather than scoring only SG-positive cells.
  • Useful conceptual control for 40S versus 60S enrichment.

Experiment idea

For drug-induced SGs with strong RPL7 enrichment, compare canonical arsenite SGs with the drug condition using both a 40S marker and a 60S marker, then ask whether the ribosomal composition predicts translation inhibition.

Nature Communications · 2025 · Tanaka et al.

Ribosome biogenesis as a potential therapeutic target in KRAS mutant colorectal cancer

Relevance to your PhD: 8.5/10

Main question

Do cancer cells become dependent on ribosome biogenesis as an adaptive response to KRAS-pathway inhibition?

Problem solved

Resistance and tolerance to targeted KRAS-pathway therapies remain major problems, and the adaptive cellular state supporting survival is incompletely understood.

Key findings

KRAS-pathway inhibition induced a state with enhanced ribosome-biogenesis gene expression in a subset of colorectal cancer cells. These cells became vulnerable to RNA polymerase I inhibition, supporting ribosome biogenesis as a therapeutically exploitable dependency.

Why it matters to your research

It connects anticancer treatment with induced dependence on ribosome production and gives you a useful framework for studying drugs that perturb nucleolar function or ribosome synthesis.

What you can learn / use

  • Think about ribosome biogenesis as an adaptive drug-response state, not only a constitutive cancer phenotype.
  • Use nucleolar/ribosome-biogenesis markers alongside cell-stress and translation readouts.
  • Consider combination-treatment logic: pathway inhibition plus ribosome-biogenesis inhibition.

Experiment idea

For your strongest SG-inducing anticancer drugs, ask whether nucleolar morphology or NPM1 localization changes together with SG formation and whether this correlates with translation inhibition.
