PhD reading rule

Do not try to memorize the whole paper.

After reading, you should be able to answer three things:
1. What did they ask?
2. What evidence supports their conclusion?
3. Does this change how I think about or design my own experiment?

Suggested reading order

Read this bench summary → inspect the key figures → read the Results around those figures → use Methods only when you need the technique → read Discussion for interpretation and limitations.
